### [concurrency] constants.BETTING["bankroll"] is mutated from three threads with no shared lock; settle does an unlocked read-modify-write
*lib/logic/engine.py:1088*

Three writers race on the same money state: (1) engine worker in _settle_round — engine.py:1087-1091 — does `BETTING["bankroll"] = max(0.0, BETTING["bankroll"] + total_eur)` under engine._lock; (2) the io-pool OCR thread in _apply_ocr_values — engine.py:1890-1891 — writes the screen balance with NO lock (the `with self._lock` at 1908 covers only bet_placed); (3) the Tk thread in modern_gui._set_bankroll — modern_gui.py:1196-1197 — writes with no lock. Because writers (2) and (3) never take engine._lock, the lock around the settle RMW protects nothing: a user edit or OCR sync landing between the settle's read and write is silently overwritten, and with auto_bankroll + sync_bankroll both on, an OCR sync of the post-payout screen balance followed by the settle increment books the payout twice (read 1050, +50 -> 1100). The corrupted value flows straight into Kelly bet sizing (betting.suggest at engine.py:2085-2089), snapshot "bankroll" (engine.py:2117), the executor's balance-mismatch guard (executor.py:487-492), and leak pricing (leaks.py:264). Note the codebase already has the correct pattern for the sibling field: engine.set_bet_placed (engine.py:1208-1214) routes the Tk write through the lock.

**Suggested fix:** Route every bankroll mutation through one engine method (e.g. engine.adjust_bankroll(delta)/set_bankroll(value)) that takes engine._lock, and have OCR sync defer to it; in the settle path, skip the increment when an OCR sync newer than the round-end already reflects the payout.

### [data-layer] Doubled hands are settled at 1 unit, so stored pnl_units/pnl_eur are systematically wrong for every double — and the error propagates into bankroll auto-update and Monte Carlo RoR
*C:/Users/mauri/Documents/python/Blackjack-AI/lib/logic/settlement.py:11-13,116-117 (also lib/logic/session_store.py:124-133,410-418)*

settlement.py's own docstring concedes 'a 3-card hand could be a double or a hit, so units are 1 per hand' — every doubled win/loss (~10% of hands) is recorded at half its real value in the rounds table (record_round stores settle['my_units']/my_eur, session_store.py:124-133). Downstream consumers treat it as truth: settled_pnl() feeds the bankroll Monte Carlo resampler (session_store.py:410-418), stats() reports net_eur, and the bankroll auto-update credits/debits the wrong amount. **Suggested fix:** Add a per-hand bet-multiplier field to settlement entries, populated from the executor's confirmed Double action when available and otherwise a one-key 'doubled' toggle on the owned seat, and settle units against it.
